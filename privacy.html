Privacy Policy

Splash Page · Effective 4 October 2026

In short: Splash Page doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal data. Your comics and reading progress stay on your device, and the app talks directly to Dropbox – never to servers of ours.

What the app stores, and where

  • Comics you download are saved on your device, inside the app. They are not included in iCloud backups and are deleted when you remove them in the app or delete the app.
  • Reading progress and settings (such as the page you are on, sort order and reading direction) are stored on your device only.
  • Your Dropbox sign-in is kept as an access token in the iOS Keychain on your device, so you stay signed in. Signing out in the app removes it.

Dropbox

When you sign in, you grant Splash Page read-only access to your Dropbox: it can list your folders and download files you choose, and it can never change or delete anything. Files go straight from Dropbox to your device. Your use of Dropbox is covered by Dropbox's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No accounts with us, and no servers of ours that receive your data.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

Splash Page doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
