Splash Page

A comic book reader for iPhone and iPad.

Splash Page reads the CBZ and CBR comics you keep in Dropbox. Browse your folders, download the issues you want, and read them offline – one page at a time on iPhone, or as two-page spreads on iPad in landscape.

  • Reads CBZ and CBR comic files
  • Browse and download from your own Dropbox, one issue or a whole folder at a time
  • Remembers your page in every comic
  • Two-page spreads on iPad, pinch and double-tap zoom
  • Right-to-left mode for manga
  • No ads, no tracking, no account with us
